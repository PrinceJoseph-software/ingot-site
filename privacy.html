Privacy policy

For the Ingot browser extension. Last updated 24 November 2026.

Ingot reads the style values of the page you are looking at, and only when you ask it to. This page lists exactly what it reads, what leaves your browser, and what it never collects.

Ingot never collects:
  • your browsing history, or the address of any page you inspect
  • page text, HTML, class names, attribute values, form contents or screenshots
  • cookies, passwords or anything you type into websites
  • analytics or tracking data. Ingot contains no analytics code.
We do not sell, rent or share data with advertisers or data brokers.

What stays on your device

Inspect mode, the quick palette, copying CSS or Tailwind classes, the eyedropper and the assets list all run inside your browser. Nothing from them is sent anywhere. Ingot only runs on a page after you click its toolbar icon, use its shortcut, or choose “Allow on every site” under Site access in Settings, which asks your browser for permission first.

Ingot keeps its settings, a random install ID, your licence key (if you enter one) and the most recent report in your browser's extension storage. Removing the extension deletes them.

What is sent, and when

Ingot talks to one server, our backend hosted on Supabase. It sends:

WhenWhat is sent
You click Run assay Style values only: colour values with a weight and an “is interactive” flag, font family names with size, line height, weight, character count and heading level, spacing and radius values with counts, and box-shadow values. No text, no HTML, no URLs, no class names, no attribute values, no screenshots. The values are refined into a report and not stored.
Every request Your install ID: a random number created when Ingot is installed, used to count the 3 free assays each month, run the 7-day trial and limit request rates. It is not linked to your name, email or browser.
You enter a licence key The key, to check it with Polar, our payment provider. We store only a one-way hash of it.
You save a kit (Pro) The kit's name, any tags you add, its design tokens, and the domain of the page it came from (for example example.com, never the full address). Saving a kit is the only time a domain is sent.
You export The kit's design tokens, to format them. The export is not stored.

What we store, and for how long

  • Install record: the install ID, when it was created, trial start and the monthly free assay count.
  • Kits: as listed above, with their last 10 versions, until you delete the kit.
  • Licence status: the hashed key, plan and renewal date, as reported by Polar.
  • Rate-limit counters: per install or licence, removed after two hours.

Payments

Ingot Pro is sold through Polar, which acts as merchant of record. Polar handles checkout, card details, tax and invoices under its own privacy policy; Ingot never sees your card. Polar tells our backend the status of your subscription or purchase and which licence key belongs to it.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database and server functions.
  • Polar processes payments and issues licence keys.

Both act on our behalf. No other party receives data from Ingot.

Your choices

  • Use Inspect mode and the quick palette without sending anything.
  • Delete any kit from the Kits tab; its versions are deleted with it.
  • Choose “Remove access” under Site access in Settings at any time to withdraw that permission.
  • Ask us to delete the records linked to your install ID or licence key by writing to the address below.

Changes

If what Ingot collects changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the new version of the extension is released.
